## Entire Market Value Rule Application

Under the **Entire Market Value Rule** (EMVR), when a trade secret "is the basis for customer demand" for an integrated product, damages may be calculated on the entire product value — not just the component parts. *See Versata Software v. SAP* (Fed. Cir.); *Bianco v. Globus Medical* (E.D. Pa.).

The **Official Bitcoin Game Show** integrates all component technologies into a single commercial product. The Fair Play Game Protocol's provably fair mechanics are **the basis for customer demand** (Twitch partnership, £14M projection). Damages may therefore be calculated on the entire integrated product value.
