## THE SELECTIVE PAYMENT ANOMALY

### March 27, 2025: CLIENT ACCOUNT GIVEN FOR COSTS PAYMENT

At the Costs and Case Management Conference (CCMC), Spector, the Claimant, was assessed £5,000 in costs. Constantine Law **provided their bank details** and accepted direct payment into their client account.

### August 8, 2025: CLIENT ACCOUNT WITHHELD FOR COSTS PAYMENT - DIRECTED TO PAY INTO SEVERINA'S PERSONAL ACCOUNT

The same firm **refused** to accept direct payment into their client account. Instead, they directed Spector to pay into Lilia Severina's personal account.

Spector was so alarmed by this irregular instruction that he created another application to the court demanding instruction from the judge. The judge instructed him to comply with the law firm's request, twice, by email.

**The Question:** Why would a law firm selectively accept or refuse direct payments into their regulated client account?

#### Possible Explanations

1. **Source of funds concerns** — Client account may have contained funds they didn't want mingled with Claimant's payment

2. **AML compliance issues** — Account may have been flagged or under review at that time

3. **Hiding payment sources** — Avoiding audit trail that would reveal who else is paying into the account

4. **Tactical litigation gamesmanship** — Creating procedural obstacles

UK solicitors' client accounts are subject to:

| Requirement | Implication |
|-------------|-------------|
| **SRA Accounts Rules** | All client money movements must be documented |
| **Source of Funds verification** | Law firm must verify where client's money comes from |
| **Anti-Money Laundering (AML)** | Suspicious transactions must be reported to NCA |
